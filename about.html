Teto Clicker

An Incremental Clicker Game

🥖 About the Game

Teto Clicker is a fan-made incremental idle game starring the beloved UTAU vocal synth Kasane Teto. Click Teto to generate Tetos, spend them on upgrades to boost your production, then Prestige to earn permanent Teto Tokens and unlock even more power across multiple runs.

This is the developer's first game project built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no frameworks needed. It started as a simple clicker and has grown to include multiple minigames, a full prestige system, permanent upgrades, an Ultimate ability, and more.

👆 Click & Idle Click Teto or let upgrades do the work for you.
⬆️ Upgrades Buy TPS & TPC upgrades that scale infinitely.
✨ Prestige Reset for Teto Tokens and permanent bonuses.
🎮 Minigames Baguette Mania, Pear Shuffle & Teto's Pond.
⚡ Ultimate Mode Charge up & unleash massive multipliers.
💾 Save / Load Import & export save codes to keep progress.
